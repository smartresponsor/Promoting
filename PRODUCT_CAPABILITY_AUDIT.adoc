= Product Capability Audit: Promoting
:revdate: 2026-09-20

== Boundary verdict

Promoting now has an executable commercial-incentive core. Pricing remains authoritative for base/contextual price selection; Carting and Ordering own their aggregates; Shipping executes shipment. Campaign remains inside Promoting rather than becoming a separate component.

== Capability matrix

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Capability |Status |Audit
|Promotion lifecycle |IMPLEMENTED |Immutable promotion definition with explicit active/inactive lifecycle.
|Promotion catalog/selection |IMPLEMENTED |Immutable catalog enforces promotion-id uniqueness and selects eligible promotions deterministically while preserving every evaluation result.
|Conditions/eligibility rules |IMPLEMENTED |Ordered deterministic minimum-subtotal and currency conditions with reason codes.
|Actions/application methods |IMPLEMENTED |Fixed and basis-point percentage monetary actions plus typed non-price benefit evaluation.
|Coupon issuance/validation |IMPLEMENTED |Immutable issuance book enforces normalized uniqueness; issue/deactivate lifecycle, customer binding, validity windows, and deterministic validation are implemented.
|Usage/per-customer limits |IMPLEMENTED |Global and per-customer coupon limits are enforced against the supplied redemption ledger.
|Date windows |IMPLEMENTED |Promotion and campaign windows are explicit and deterministic; promotion evaluation requires caller-supplied time when a window exists.
|Priority/stacking/exclusivity |IMPLEMENTED |Priority-descending/id-ascending resolution with explicit stackable/exclusive policy.
|Campaign grouping |IMPLEMENTED |Campaigns group promotion identifiers and support activate/pause/resume lifecycle.
|Campaign budgets |IMPLEMENTED |Cumulative spend is bounded and budget exhaustion is evaluated deterministically.
|Buy-X-Get-Y/free gift |IMPLEMENTED |Typed entitlement facts preserve qualifying/reward SKU and quantity semantics.
|Free shipping incentive |IMPLEMENTED |Promoting emits free-shipping eligibility only; Shipping retains shipment execution ownership.
|Redemption ledger/reversal |PARTIAL |Immutable idempotent redeem/reverse semantics are implemented; durable concurrent persistence remains an integration concern.
|Evaluation explanation |IMPLEMENTED |Eligibility, application, coupon, campaign, and benefit paths expose stable reason codes.
|===

== Milestone direction

Milestone 1: implemented and verified.
Milestone 2: implemented core; durable coupon issuance/redemption persistence remains follow-up integration work.
Milestone 3: implemented and verified, including campaign and promotion-level validity windows.
Milestone 4: promotion-side BXGY/free-gift/free-shipping semantics implemented; direct Carting/Ordering consumption remains a sibling integration task.
Milestone 5: coupon issuance lifecycle, audience binding, uniqueness, and coupon validity windows implemented and verified.
Milestone 6: immutable promotion catalog and deterministic eligible selection implemented.
